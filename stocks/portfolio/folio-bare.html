Reading this chart: one grouped pair per trading day. 2026-08-12 through 08-17 are a one-time backfill from IBKR's own NAV history; 2026-08-18 onward is the daily scheduled pull, one new pair added each weekday. The deposited bar stays flat across dates until the manual figure above is next updated — it isn't pulled daily the way NLV is.
What "bare" means: this page shows only what IBKR's account API returns directly (net liquidation value, cash, position market value, unrealized P&L) — refreshed once a day by a scheduled pull, no browser fetch, no client-side caching logic needed. It deliberately omits XIRR, TWR, and the deposit/cost-basis reconciliation that FOLIO 2 has — those need either full transaction history or a manual dashboard read, not just an account-summary pull.
